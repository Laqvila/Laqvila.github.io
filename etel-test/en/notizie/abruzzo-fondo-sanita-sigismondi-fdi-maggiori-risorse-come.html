From the Territory December 5, 2023 · Press release

Abruzzo - healthcare fund, Sigismondi (FdI): More resources, as never before, for even more efficient healthcare

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

“The Abruzzo Region achieves another historic result that will allow us to continue laying the foundations to build efficient and modern healthcare. Today, Undersecretary Morelli announced that Cipess has allocated to our region about two and a half billion euros from the National Health Fund; a more than considerable increase, given that it amounts to over 75 million more than in 2022. The resources made available by the National Health Fund are remarkable, as never before in these years, and will make it possible to cope with the higher costs the system has to bear. This is a recognition of the work carried out by the Marsilio administration which, despite having inherited an almost disastrous situation, has succeeded in raising efficiency levels, guaranteeing better services for citizens. And it is along this path that the work will continue, strengthened by the support of the national government, which demonstrates, once again, that it keeps the commitments made to the electorate, confirming substantial investments in Healthcare as well.” Thus the Abruzzo senator of Fratelli d’Italia Etelwardo Sigismondi.
