From the Territory July 3, 2023 · Press release

Pescara: Sigismondi on the Ferro di Cavallo, a symbol of the city's underworld falls

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

As of today, the symbol and the stronghold of the Pescara underworld, and beyond, falls. With the start of the demolition operations on the ‘Ferro di Cavallo’, the true phase of rebirth begins for an outlying neighbourhood that for too many years was at the centre of regional and national news reports and where, thanks to the tireless work carried out by the law enforcement agencies, numerous criminals were found in that den. I recall how the most recent raid led to the arrest of 20 people who will have to answer, among other offences, for mafia-style criminal association. I express satisfaction with a great team effort by Marco Marsilio's Abruzzo Region, by Carlo Masci's Municipality of Pescara and by Ater which, from 2019 to today, has planned and carried out targeted activities to combat illegal occupations, decay and crime. A complex effort that today sees a decisive step accomplished, paving the way for the redevelopment of the entire neighbourhood. This is serious politics, which plans, builds and creates well-being
