From Parliament March 2, 2023 · Press release

Reconstruction Decree, Sigismondi (FdI): Satisfaction with the approval in the Senate. A text drafted with care and seriousness

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

“As Rapporteur of the measure, I express great satisfaction with today's approval in the Senate of the text converting the Reconstruction decree into law, which demonstrates the great sensitivity that the Meloni Government has toward the territories hit by the earthquake and their citizens. My thanks go, therefore, to President Meloni, to the Minister for Civil Protection, Musumeci, to the Extraordinary Commissioner for Reconstruction, Castelli, also for the work carried out in synergy with the 8th Committee, to the President of that same Committee, Fazzone, as well as to all the colleagues who showed attention to one of the most important issues for our Nation”.

This is what the Abruzzo senator, Etelwardo Sigismondi, group leader of Fratelli d'Italia in the Committee on the Environment, Ecological Transition, Energy, Public Works, Communications, Technological Innovation, declared in the Chamber. “There are many significant aspects contained in the document, all geared toward accelerating and simplifying public reconstruction in the areas hit by seismic events, with rules useful for unblocking the construction sites still at a standstill – Sigismondi stressed again – and many are the topics addressed, including the stabilization of personnel, through the reopening of the deadlines for accruing the requirements for stabilization by December 31, 2023, which makes it possible not to lose valuable professional resources. Another significant measure concerns the advance of the VAT for invoices relating to reconstruction works, with the provision authorizing the extraordinary commissioner to disburse it in order to address the financial difficulties of businesses.

“In addition, the derogation from the minimum number of pupils per class was approved, a rule that will make it possible not to alter the current school structure of these fragile territories. Important measures also for the municipalities of the island of Ischia – Sigismondi continues – such as the Government's amendment establishing the criteria and methods for disbursing the resources to compensate, in the municipalities of Casamicciola Terme and Lacco Ameno, for the lower revenues due to the suspension of taxes”.

“Again for the municipalities of the island of Ischia, there is the possibility of hiring on permanent contracts, for the reconstruction following the 2017 earthquake, the personnel recruited through competitive or public selection procedures and currently serving on fixed-term contracts. Still other measures concern the possibility of reserving 30 percent of the posts in recruitment competitions for permanent employment for personnel falling within the category of orphans and spouses of the victims of the seismic events, in addition to the refinancing of the litigation fund, which will allow compensation for the pupils who suffered injuries in the collapse of the school of S. Giuliano di Puglia in 2002. A text drafted with care, seriousness and a great sense of responsibility, in order to be as complete and effective as possible”, concluded Sigismondi.
