From the Territory July 22, 2025 · Press release

Justice returns to Abruzzo, the Meloni government signs the rescue of the sub-provincial courts”

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

“Excellent news for Abruzzo. The Council of Ministers today approved the bill for the reorganization of the judicial geography, which enshrines the definitive stabilization of the courts of Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto. This is a measure awaited for years, which demonstrates once again how the government led by Giorgia Meloni keeps the commitments made to the citizens of Abruzzo. The path undertaken is exactly the one announced during the meeting we promoted last November, when Undersecretary Andrea Delmastro met the coordination of the mayors and the presidents of the Bar Associations, and subsequently reiterated in May at the meeting with the Minister of Justice Carlo Nordio: on the one hand, the extension of the courts’ operation to guarantee their operational continuity; on the other, a government-initiated bill to enshrine the definitive rescue of our sub-provincial courts. As members of Parliament from Abruzzo, from the very first day of the legislature we have worked in synergy with the Meloni Government, Minister Nordio, Undersecretary Delmastro and the President of the Region Marco Marsilio to achieve this historic milestone. It is worth emphasizing that never, until today, had a government adopted a legislative measure of its own to safeguard our courts. The facts refute the polemics raised by opposition members of Parliament, who until a few days ago questioned the effectiveness of our work and the correctness of the path undertaken. It is worth remembering that, in the periods when the Democratic Party and the 5 Star Movement were in government, they never managed to put in place concrete acts to guarantee the definitive rescue of the Abruzzo courts, limiting themselves to simple annual extensions. And yet, over these years, they have even had the presumption to criticize our work. Today, with the Meloni government and Fratelli d’Italia, the commitments made to the territory are being kept thanks to the launch of the process for the definitive rescue of the courts of Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto. We now hope that all parliamentary forces will cooperate to guarantee a swift process, so that the bill is approved as quickly as possible.” This was stated, in a joint note, by the Abruzzo members of Parliament of Fratelli d’Italia Etelwardo Sigismondi, Guido Liris and Guerino Testa.
