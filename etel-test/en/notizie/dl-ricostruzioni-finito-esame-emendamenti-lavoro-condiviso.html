From Parliament February 25, 2023 · Press release

Reconstruction Decree: Examination of amendments completed, shared work

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

“The examination of the amendments to the text of the implementing decree of the Reconstruction decree in the Senate's 8th Committee on the Environment, Ecological Transition, Energy, Public Works, Communications and Technological Innovation has been completed. The work carried out by the Committee and by the majority and opposition groups was a shared effort, which made it possible to gather the requests of the bodies involved and of the various actors of the Reconstruction. Special thanks go to the Extraordinary Commissioner for Reconstruction, Senator Guido Castelli, for the important contribution made to the text and also for the valuable coordination with the Committee itself so that the document would meet the needs of the territories hit by the earthquake”.

This is stated by the Fratelli d'Italia senator, Etelwardo Sigismondi, rapporteur of the decree. “The decree, adopted by the Meloni government last January 11 – he explains – shows the great attention devoted to an issue that is important for our Nation and for post-earthquake reconstruction. During the conversion process, significant additions were made, including the extension, also to the territories hit by the 2016/2017 seismic crisis, of the acceleration and simplification measures already provided for reconstruction works in the municipalities affected by the 2009 earthquake. As announced, particular commitment was devoted to the professionals of the municipalities and of the Special Offices employed in the Reconstruction, with the possibility of stabilizing the temporary personnel involved, also so as not to disperse the wealth of knowledge acquired.

The text was also further enriched by measures that allow municipalities to unblock the construction sites still at a standstill.
Another important rule – Sigismondi points out – is the one providing for the possibility of reserving up to 30% of the posts in competitions, for non-managerial personnel, for the orphans and spouses of the victims of the 2009 and 2016 earthquakes. Also significant is the regulatory provision aimed at addressing the financial difficulties of businesses relating to the works covered by contributions for the reconstruction of the buildings damaged by the 2016 earthquake and pertaining to the business activity itself, and to this end the extraordinary commissioner is given the possibility of disbursing advances on the VAT”. “Also worthy of note are the ‘school amendment’ – a measure long proposed, also in past legislatures, by MP Trancassini and designed to ward off depopulation and support the recovery of social life in small communities – which allows, in the municipalities affected by the earthquake and by the hydrogeological instability of the island of Ischia, a derogation from the minimum number of pupils per class, and the ‘S. Giuliano di Puglia Molise earthquake amendment’ which provides for compensation for the pupils who suffered injuries in the collapse of the school following the earthquake in 2002”, concludes the FdI senator.
