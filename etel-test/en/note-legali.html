Legal notes & Privacy

Transparency on the nature of the website, data processing, cookies and the use of artificial intelligence.

Nature of the website

This page is published as an unofficial working preview, created for the review of its content together with the staff of Senator Etelwardo Sigismondi, ahead of its possible publication on the official domain etelwardosigismondi.it. It is neither an institutional website of the Senate of the Republic nor an official website of the Senator until further notice.

Cookies and tracking tools

This website does not use any cookies — neither technical nor profiling ones — and does not integrate analytics, tracking or profiling tools. Under the applicable legislation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679 — GDPR, the ePrivacy Directive and the Italian Data Protection Authority's Guidelines of 10 June 2021), no consent banner is required: there is no processing to consent to.

The fonts are hosted directly on this website: visiting it generates no requests to third-party servers (for example Google Fonts) and no browsing data — including the IP address — is disclosed to third parties.

Processing of personal data

The website is static and does not collect data: the form on the Contacts page merely composes an email in the visitor's email client, without sending or storing anything on this website. Communications addressed to the institutional email are handled according to the rules of the Senate of the Republic.

Transparency on the use of artificial intelligence

In accordance with the transparency principles of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act), please note that this website was designed and developed with the assistance of artificial intelligence tools (Anthropic's Claude assistant), under the direction and review of a human curator. The content is drawn from verifiable public sources and is subject to human review before final publication; translations are generated with AI and reviewed. The website does not employ any AI systems in operation (chatbots, algorithmic profiling or automated decision-making processes).

Content sources and press releases

The biographical and institutional content is drawn from verifiable public sources: the Senate of the Republic, the Chamber of Deputies, Openpolis. The press releases in the "From Parliament" and "From the Territory" sections are reproduced in full from the archive already published by the Senator and his staff on the previous official website; versions in languages other than Italian are unofficial translations.

Images

The photographs and the "L'Abruzzo in Parlamento" graphics are taken from materials already published on the Senator's official channels.

Reports

To report inaccuracies or request changes: etelwardo.sigismondi@senato.it.
