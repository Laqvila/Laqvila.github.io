From the Territory July 28, 2023 · Press release

Pescara-Roma: While the project takes shape thanks to the centre-right, the PD should say mea culpa

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

The culpable delays and the obstruction attempts of the centre-left count for nothing against the incisiveness of Marsilio and of the current national government”.

I have the distinct impression that while the centre-right, from the national government to the regional one, is working to deliver the speed upgrade of the Pescara-Roma railway line, in the centre-left, hiding behind an apparent alarmism over financial coverage, many are rooting against the completion of the project, showing their usual allergy to turning infrastructure into reality. Unfortunately for them, there is no abandonment of the project by the national government, which considers the speed upgrade of the Pescara – Roma line a priority, as reiterated at the important meeting last Monday in Avezzano, organised by President Marsilio, and in recent hours both by Infrastructure Minister Salvini and by Deputy Minister Bignami.

The lengthy authorisation process made it necessary to rework the form of financing which, as pointed out by Deputy Minister Bignami himself, will be covered with funds other than the PNRR. So the speed-upgrade project moves forward. The project, considered unfeasible for years, took concrete shape only thanks to the incisiveness of President Marco Marsilio: in March 2020, just one year after he took office, the memorandum of understanding was signed between the Ministry of Transport, the Abruzzo Region and the Lazio Region and, subsequently, thanks to the establishment of the ensuing technical working group, the financial requirements for the project were identified. The PD parliamentarians, who maliciously try to attribute responsibility to the Abruzzo Region, would do well to say mea culpa for the delays with which their national government appointed the current Extraordinary Commissioner and for the attitude of their local administrators who, in order to block the project, did not hesitate to appeal to the Regional Administrative Court.

We could, moreover, list the critical issues left by the centre-left both in the drafting and in the management of the National Recovery and Resilience Plan, which led the Meloni government and the competent Minister to revise the Plan. The political controversy staged by the centre-left therefore appears sterile and groundless. The work of the Meloni government, the Marsilio regional government and the Abruzzo parliamentarians of Fratelli d'Italia is focused on achieving the objectives of the PNRR, ensuring that all the works originally planned are carried out, starting of course with the Pescara-Roma line”
