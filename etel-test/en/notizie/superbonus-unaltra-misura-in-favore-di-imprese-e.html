From Parliament March 30, 2023 · Press release

Superbonus, another measure in favor of businesses and territories. The center-left proven wrong. Work continues to repair the serious damage caused by previous governments

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

The green light given yesterday by the Finance Committee of the Chamber to the decree on Superbonus credits is excellent news. The approval of the amendment providing for the derogation on the transfer of credits in all the areas of central Italy hit by the earthquakes and by the 2022 flood represents another concrete step taken for the revival of these territories and attests, once again, that the Meloni government keeps the commitments it has made. An important response that demolishes the false accusations of the center-left, whose representatives took turns claiming that we would not take into account the needs of the territories, first on the Reconstruction decree and then on the complicated issue of the Superbonus. Proven wrong by the facts. The truth is different: this government set to work immediately to remedy the messes created by the previous administrations, led by the M5S, and to redress, with serious and effective measures, the severe economic difficulties that brought Italian businesses to the brink of bankruptcy because of stranded credits, the result of chaotic legislation
