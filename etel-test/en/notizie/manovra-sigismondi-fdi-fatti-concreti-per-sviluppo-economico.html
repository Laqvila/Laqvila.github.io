From Parliament December 21, 2024 · Press release

Budget, Sigismondi (FdI): Concrete facts for economic development, support for families and the weakest

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

“This year’s budget law reaffirms our commitment, in continuity with the previous budget packages, to focus on economic development, on support for families, on workers, on businesses and on the weakest. This is the third budget law drawn up by the Meloni government and it is the first to deal with the new rules of the Stability Pact. But unlike in the past, when resources were wasted, with this government they have been allocated to the fundamental sectors.” Thus, during the general debate in the chamber, the Abruzzo senator of Fratelli d’Italia Etel Sigismondi. “Healthcare has been allocated 136.5 billion, raising per capita health spending from 1,900 euros in 2018 to the current 2,300 euros. The cut in the tax wedge, which amounts to 17 billion, has brought an increase in workers’ pay packets of an extra 100 euros a month, while the extension of the guarantee fund will bring support to small and medium-sized enterprises, the economic engine of the Nation. Moreover, with the premium Ires, 18 thousand companies will be able to benefit from a 4% tax cut, which will generate new investments and new hiring. And then support for families and the birth rate, the increase in minimum pensions and the measures for the South. Concrete and tangible facts that refute all the fake news of the oppositions, from whom we certainly accept no lessons,” Sigismondi concludes.
