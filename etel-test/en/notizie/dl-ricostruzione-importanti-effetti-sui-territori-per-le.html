From the Territory August 31, 2023 · Press release

Reconstruction Decree: Important effects on the territories. Over 528 thousand euros allocated to the Abruzzo schools of the crater area. Still strong signals from the Meloni Government for the earthquake areas.

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

The Reconstruction Decree produces important effects on the territories struck by the earthquake and on citizens, starting with the schools falling within the crater area. With an amendment that I presented as rapporteur of the decree, and strongly supported by the Extraordinary Commissioner for Reconstruction, Guido Castelli, provision has in fact been made for the schools of the 2016 crater to form classes even in derogation from the minimum number of students established by law, up until the 2028/2029 school year.

An important amendment that makes it possible to preserve the current school arrangement in these fragile areas. The Ministry of Education and Merit, by its own decree, has authorized and financed the formation of the classes, allocating to the Regional School Offices about 3.5 million for the staff to be employed in the 2023/2024 school year, now upon us.

Specifically, the sums allocated to the regions are as follows: Abruzzo € 528,184.41, Lazio € 365,666.13, Marche € 2,072,108.07 and Umbria € 609,443.55.

From the Meloni government, once again, strong signals of attention and closeness to the areas struck by the earthquake.
