From Parliament March 21, 2023 · Press release

Territories at the center for a concrete change of pace with the Meloni Government

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

“The Reconstruction decree is a concrete response to the real needs of the territories hit by the earthquake. The citizens and public administrations of those territories can today count on a legislative text, wanted by the Meloni government, which sets a very clear objective: to speed up the reconstruction processes in order to recover lost time, moving from a planning phase to the opening of construction sites. One of the main regulatory strategies put in place is called ‘simplification’, to overcome bureaucratic obstacles and to ensure faster procedures and certainty over the timeframes of the works for recovery. A comprehensive and far-sighted measure that testifies to this government's determination to bring a tangible change of pace to the process of reconstruction and revival of the seismic crater of Central Italy”.

This is what the Abruzzo senator of Fratelli d'Italia and group leader in the 7th Committee in the Senate, Etelwardo Sigismondi, declared yesterday morning on the sidelines of the press conference held by Minister Musumeci and the Extraordinary Commissioner for Reconstruction, Castelli.
