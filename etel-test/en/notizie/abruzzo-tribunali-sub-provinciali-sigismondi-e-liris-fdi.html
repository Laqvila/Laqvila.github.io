From the Territory January 10, 2025 · Press release

Abruzzo - sub-provincial courts, Sigismondi and Liris (FdI): Clear will for a definitive reopening

Translation of the official press release published in Italian.

“It is true, the Milleproroghe decree does not include the extension for the courts of Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto. It does not include it, not out of forgetfulness but out of a clear will to provide a definitive solution for the reopening of the Abruzzo sub-provincial courts. It must be acknowledged, in fact, that while on the one hand the extensions have made it possible to keep these outposts of justice alive, on the other, after thirteen years, the time has come for structured choices that resolve the many critical issues that have emerged over these years, starting with the staffing plans of the administrative personnel. Just yesterday, in fact, the Undersecretary for Justice Andrea Delmastro, answering a parliamentary question in the Senate chamber, announced the presentation by the Government of a draft bill for the revision of the judicial geography, with the reopening of some offices abolished in 2012. The Undersecretary’s answer is in line with what emerged from the meeting that we, the Fratelli d’Italia members of Parliament, organized in November between the coordination of the mayors and the presidents of the Bar Associations of Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto, together with Undersecretary Delmastro himself. This year, therefore, the aim is the definitive rescue of the Abruzzo sub-provincial courts and not the mere extension of their functions. Should the timeline for the approval of the bill suffer delays, we are ready to intervene with further extensions. We are in constant contact with the Ministry of Justice, local administrators and the representatives of the bar associations to inform them of and share the various stages that will lead to the safeguarding of our courts.” Thus, in a note, the Fratelli d’Italia senators Etelwardo Sigismondi and Guido Quintino Liris
L’Aquila, 10 January 2025
