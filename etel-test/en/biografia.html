Biography

An architect by training, serving Abruzzo's institutions for over twenty-five years.

Who he is

An architect by training, an Abruzzese from Vasto, among the founders of Fratelli d'Italia in Abruzzo.

From the territory to Palazzo Madama

Born in Vasto on 29 September 1974, he graduated in Architecture from the "G. D'Annunzio" University of Chieti-Pescara. His political commitment began at a very young age in the Fronte della Gioventù and continued in Azione Giovani, the youth organisation of Alleanza Nazionale, where he held provincial, regional and national roles: in 2004 he joined the National Executive chaired by Giorgia Meloni.

He rose through twenty years of local government — municipal councillor in Vasto, provincial councillor in Chieti — up to the post of head of the secretariat of the President of the Abruzzo Region, Marco Marsilio. In 2012 he was among the founders of Fratelli d'Italia in Abruzzo, serving as its regional coordinator since 2018. Since 13 October 2022 he has been a Senator of the Republic for the Abruzzo constituency.
