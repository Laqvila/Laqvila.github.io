Legislative initiatives

Bills as first signatory

DDL S. 1877

National monument for the victims of Marcinelle

Declares Piazza Caduti di Marcinelle and the Chapel of the Victims in Manoppello (PE) a national monument, in memory of the 262 miners who died in 1956 — 22 of them from Manoppello. Approved on the 70th anniversary of the tragedy.

Approved unanimously by the Senate · under examination by the Chamber of Deputies
DDL S. 1458

Recognition of clown therapy

Provisions for the recognition and promotion of "smile therapy" as a psychosocial support activity in healthcare settings.

Under examination
DDL S. 877

Protection of traditional fishing

Safeguarding and promotion of the traditional fishing gear and techniques of Italy's maritime heritage, such as the trabocchi of the Abruzzo coast.
